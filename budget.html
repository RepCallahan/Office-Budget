Monthly update

Paste the Apps Script web app URL (it should look like https://script.google.com/macros/s/…/exec). Each time you open Office Budget — and after you add, edit, or delete an expense — a snapshot of the current month is saved. On the last day of the month, Apps Script emails that snapshot. Expenses entered after the last open will not be in the email until someone opens the app. Use Send update now to email the current month right away.

Office categories

If the office category list is missing or incomplete, restore it. Categories you already have are kept and not duplicated. Starting spend is filled in only on categories that have no spending yet.

Archive

Fold logged expenses into Already spent and clear the list. Totals stay the same. Use this if storage is getting full.

Expenses are stored on a Trello card named “Budget Storage 1 – Don't delete (budget data)”. Add that card once, then reopen Office Budget. You can archive the card so it stays off the board — just don’t delete it. If one card fills up, add “Budget Storage 2 – Don't delete (budget data)” the same way.
