Extra storage cards must stay in an open list on the board (collapse the list if you like). Never archive the list or the cards: Power-Ups cannot see archived cards, and your documents would seem to disappear. Never delete them.

About

Documents live on Google Drive. This app only keeps the titles, links, and signatures, and stores them on this Trello board. Staff see only their own signature. That is hidden from view, not encrypted: someone technical with board access could read the raw Trello data.
